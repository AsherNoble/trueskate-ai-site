04THE HAND

Most robotic hands in the literature are designed to grip and manipulate objects, which means they are optimised for torque rather than speed. Mine needs relatively little torque but requires very high speed, acceleration and precision. I’m basing my design on the open-source orcahand v2 – with gimbal motors instead of high-torque servos – and intend to make hardware modifications until it is optimised for virtual finger skating.

DoF18 (3 per finger, 4 in the thumb, 2 in the wrist)
DriveTendon-driven
