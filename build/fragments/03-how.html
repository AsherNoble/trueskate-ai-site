03HOW IT WORKS

The protoype of this project used covariance matrix adaptation evolution strategy (CMA-ES) – a sample-efficient RL approach – with the on-screen trick name as the reward signal. The project is now being built around two-stage behavioral cloning, outlined below:

01demonstrationsexpert demonstrations of my True Skate gameplay are screen-recorded and saved.
02gesture corpusa script performs random gestures via Appium + WebDriverAgent and collects the video frames they produce.
03trace extractora small U-Net is trained to predict the gestures that produced a given sequence of video frames for the corpus created in step 02.
04policya transformer (or another architecture) is trained to take recent frames plus recent gestures and predict the next few gestures.
05inferencegestures are inferred and translated from 2D gestures to 3D trajectories executed by the robotic hand on the iPhone screen.

An earlier CMA-ES baseline and a collapsed PPO + HER attempt taught me what this noisy real-time setting could and couldn’t give back.
