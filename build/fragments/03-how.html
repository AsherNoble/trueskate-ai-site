03HOW IT WORKS

The prototype of this project used covariance matrix adaptation evolution strategy (CMA-ES) – a sample-efficient RL approach – with the on-screen trick name as the reward signal. The project is now being built around two-stage behavioral cloning, outlined below:

DemonstrationsExpert demonstrations of my True Skate gameplay are screen-recorded and saved.
Data collectionA self-supervised pipeline generates and executes random synthetic touches on two iPhones plugged into my homelab (see Fig. 3), and records the resulting gameplay.
Inverse dynamics modelA spatiotemporal CNN is trained on the corpus from step 02 to reconstruct the gestures that produced a given sequence of video frames. It is then used to label the expert demonstrations from step 01.
PolicyA CNN–Transformer is trained through behavioural cloning to predict upcoming gestures from recent gameplay frames and previous gestures.
InferenceThe policy predicts gestures in real time, which are converted into 3D trajectories and executed by the robotic hand using inverse kinematics.
